Which look is the default?

Same layout A, two palettes. My suggestion is that the viewer follows the host's light/dark setting automatically (and the OS setting in the standalone window). Your pick here is the look it uses when no theme is reported, and the one I tune first.

Light studio

Shapr3D's signature look. Pale gradient backdrop, white floating cards, the model reads as a soft-shaded object.

Dark studio

Charcoal backdrop, raised dark cards, light model. Closer to the current viewer and to Shapr3D's dark mode; easier on the eyes in a dark chat host.
